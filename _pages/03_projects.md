### 2. Reducing the Noise of a Quantum Clock using Coherent Feedback

- __What?__ What was the effect of the noise when we are measuring time? What are the ultimate limits of precision when it comes to time measurement? What is the minimum amount of noise one can introduce in a clock system? With these questions in mind... <br>
- __My Role:__ I was the main responsible to execute the project. Simulations were done using Mathematica (where solving partial differential equations was required) and QuTiP (Quantum Toolbox in Python) for solving stochastic differential equations. The results are available on chapters 6 and 7 of my PhD thesis. <br>
- __With?__ This project was supervised by Prof. Gerard Milburn. <br>

### 3. Characterisation of Transition Edge-Sensors (TES)

- __What?__ The goal of this project was to characterise the operation of photon-counting detector with real time capabilities. The superconducting transition-edge sensor (TES) is a photon detector known for its high efficiency and its capabilities of counting the numbers of photons present in a detection event. In this project, we used a TES developed by our collaborators in NIST, and Dr. Geoff Gillet programmed a FPGA capable of extract and analyse the detector data, providing photon-number information in real time. <br>
- __My Role:__ In this project, I calibrated and characterised the detectors, wrote the documentation for using the detectors at QT Lab, wrote the tespackage a python code to perform calibration and measurements with the TES detectors, and wrote the paper. If you want more info, you can read the first 5 chapters of my PhD thesis, which discuss the project with much more detail. <br>
-    __With?__ This project was supervised by Prof. Andrew G. White, Dr. Marcelo Pereira Almeida, and Dr. Till Weinhold. Also collaborated in this project Dr. Joshua Combes, Dr. Geoff Gillett, Dr. Markus Rambach, Dr. Sae Woo Nam, Dr. Thomas Gerrits, and Dr. Adriana Lita. <br>
-    __Where?__ The University of Queensland - Brisbane, Australia <br>
-    __This research was supported by:__ Programa Doutorado Pleno no Exterior (DPE) 88881.128437/2016-01 da CAPES and Australian Research Council Centre of Excellence for Engineered Quantum Systems (EQUS, CE170100009).<br>

### 4. Weak Measurements and the Double-Slit Experiment

- __What?__ Proposed by Aharonov, Albert, and Vaidman the weak measurement protocol is a quantum measurement protocol that gives a weak value as the measurement result. The protocol allows the study of the interaction between the measurement instrument (described as a classical system) and the quantum system. This project goal was to understand the weak measurement protocol, studying its impacts in the quantum mechanics interpretations and its applications, with special interest in the double-slit experiment. <br>
-    __My Role:__ Write a review on the weak measurement protocol and reproduce the results of [Observing the Average Trajectories of Single Photons in a Two-Slit Interferometer](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1202218) using simulations on MATLAB. <br>
-    __With?__ This project was supervised by [Prof. Leonardo Teixeira Neves](http://lattes.cnpq.br/0525343899407081). <br>
-    __Where?__ Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais (UFMG) - Belo Horizonte, Brazil <br>
-    __This research was supported by:__ Conselho Nacional de Desenvolvimento Científico e Tecnológico (CNPq)<br>

### 6. Nanomagnetism in low dimensions

- __What?__ In this project, we studied the thermodynamics of magnetic nanoislands with particular interest in geometric frustration, collective excitations and the rich dynamics that could emerge from these situations.   <br>
- __My Role:__ I simulated unidirectional lattices of nanoislands using Monte Carlo simulations written in Fortran. The results from my research were included in my monograph (available upon request) and published in [this paper](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/0957-4484/26/29/295303/meta) (which also featured an experimental realization of the lattices I simulated. Pretty nice, huh?) <br>
- __With?__ This project was supervised by Prof. Afranio Pereira Rodrigues and Prof. Lucas Mól. Also collaborated Prof. Winder Moura-Melo, Prof. Rodrigo Costa Silva, Prof. Clodoaldo I. L. de Araujo, Dr. Fabio Nascimento, and Dr. Renan Loreto. <br>
