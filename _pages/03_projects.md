## Past Projects

### 1. Building a Vibrational Sample Magnetometer (VSM)

- __What?__ During an exchange year at Durham University, I did a summer project in which I build a vibrational sample magnetometer (VSM) from scratch. Supervised by Dr. Ian Terry, this was my first time undertaking an experimental project. Within 3 months, we were able to build the VSM and even make some useful measurements for people from the Chemistry department! <br>
-    __My Role:__ Build the VSM, characterise its response, and perform magnetisation measurements from samples. <br>
-    __With?__ This project was supervised by Dr. Ian Terry. <br>
-    __Where?__ Durham University - Durham, United Kingdom <br>
-    __This research was supported by:__ Science without Borders (SwB) Scholarship - 203820/2013-4.

### 2. Nanomagnetism in low dimensions

- __What?__ In this project, we studied the thermodynamics of magnetic nanoislands with particular interest in geometric frustration, collective excitations and the rich dynamics that could emerge from these situations.   <br>
- __My Role:__ I simulated unidirectional lattices of nanoislands using Monte Carlo simulations written in Fortran. The results from my research were included in my monograph (available upon request) and published in [this paper](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/0957-4484/26/29/295303/meta) (which also featured an experimental realization of the lattices I simulated. Pretty nice, huh?) <br>
- __With?__ This project was supervised by Prof. Afranio Pereira Rodrigues and Prof. Lucas Mól. Also collaborated Prof. Winder Moura-Melo, Prof. Rodrigo Costa Silva, Prof. Clodoaldo I. L. de Araujo, Dr. Fabio Nascimento, and Dr. Renan Loreto. <br>
- __Where?__ Universidade Federal de Viçosa (UFV) - Viçosa, Brazil <br>
- __This research was supported by:__ Conselho Nacional de Desenvolvimento Científico e Tecnológico (CNPq).
